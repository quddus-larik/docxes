# Building a Developer Portfolio

A portfolio is more than a resume — it's proof of your craft. Here's what I learned building mine.

## Start with Content

Design without content is decoration. Write your projects, posts, and bio first, then shape the layout around them.

## Keep It Fast

- Static generation wherever possible
- Optimized images and fonts
- Minimal client-side JavaScript

## Show, Don't Tell

Link to live demos and repositories. Real, working code speaks louder than adjectives.

## Conclusion

Ship early, iterate often, and let your work do the talking.
